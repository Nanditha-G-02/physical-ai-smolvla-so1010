## Rules

- Keep each image under ~1 MB (resize to ~1600 px wide); `.png`, `.jpg` and `.svg` are allowed by `.gitignore`.
- Do not commit videos; link to them instead.
- Check that no screen capture shows a token, an email address you don't want public, or other private data.
