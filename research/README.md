## How sources were checked

- **Checked online on 20 Oct 2026:** SmolVLA (arXiv abstract and HTML), LeRobot (arXiv and ICLR 2026 listing), Kress-Gazit et al. (arXiv), Lin et al. (arXiv and ICLR 2025), STEP (arXiv), the official LeRobot SmolVLA and dataset-v3 docs, ROBOTIS Physical AI Tools (GitHub), and the Hugging Face dataset pages.
- **Well-established references** (RT-1, RT-2, ACT, Diffusion Policy, OpenVLA, π0, DAgger, LoRA, flow matching and others) are given with their arXiv IDs or DOIs from the author's knowledge and are marked as such in `references.bib`. Re-check them before formal academic use.
- Numbers quoted from papers are the authors' reported results, not reproductions.
- Summaries are paraphrased; no abstracts are reproduced.
